Summary: The Animal Sounds application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It is 100% offline, contains zero advertisements, and requires zero device permissions.

1. Introduction

Green Mouse ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data privacy for the Animal Sounds mobile application for Android devices.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information or non-personal information when you use our application. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID, or MAC address).
  • We do not collect location information (neither coarse nor precise GPS location).
  • We do not collect usage analytics, crash logs, or telemetry data.

3. 100% Offline Application

The Animal Sounds application operates entirely offline on your device. All media assets, graphics, and audio recordings are bundled locally within the app installation package. The app does not connect to the internet, does not communicate with any external servers, and does not require an active network connection to function.

4. Permissions

The application requires zero (0) permissions:

  • No INTERNET permission.
  • No ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission.
  • No storage, camera, microphone, or contact permissions.

5. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Animal Sounds is completely ad-free. There are no banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded videos, or third-party advertising SDKs integrated into the app. There are also no third-party tracking, analytics, or social media libraries.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Animal Sounds is designed for children and families. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, our app is fully compliant with:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The European General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR-K)
  • Google Play's Designed for Families policy requirements

Parents and guardians can have complete confidence that their children's privacy is fully respected and safeguarded.

7. Security

Because the app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on remote servers, your information is never at risk of interception, unauthorized access, or server-side security breaches.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
